## Work

### [Solandra](https://solandra.io)

> AI support infrastructure that turns customer conversations and documentation into actionable product intelligence

Built around multiple **RAGs** (Tree & Graph based etc.), Solandra combines retrieval, documentation intelligence, conversation analysis, and support workflows to help teams understand not just what customers are asking, but **where their product and documentation are failing them**.

`TypeScript` `Next.js` `PostgreSQL` `Qdrant` `Redis` `RAG` `AI Agents`

> Retrieval architecture that indexes generated questions instead of raw document chunks

Cosine similarity **0.71 → 0.94** · **75%+ token reduction** · Independently validated by a Seoul National University research team - [arXiv:2508.09755](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.09755)

`Python` `Qdrant` `OpenAI` `RAG`

### [PixEarn](https://pixearn.com) *(closed)*

> Creator-first image sharing platform - built and operated solo

Reached **19k+ MAU** with 1.2M+ monthly database queries at sub-150ms latency, while running at approximately **$38/month in infrastructure costs** with integrated Razorpay payments.

Closed in 2026 because I hadn't built a sustainable monetization model around the product, despite achieving meaningful usage.

`Next.js` `PostgreSQL` `Node.js` `TypeScript` `Railway`

### [CreatorJot](https://creatorjot.vercel.app) *(closed)*

> Multi-LLM ETL pipeline for YouTube content transformation

Built a pipeline coordinating **GPT, Claude, and Gemini** through pg-boss queues across a 5-node Railway deployment. Schema v2 preserved 6 fields across regeneration calls to prevent hallucination caused by partial context.

Closed after validating the product with early users and deciding to focus engineering time on **Solandra and AI infrastructure**.

`TypeScript` `pg-boss` `Railway` `OpenAI` `Claude` `Gemini`
